# World of Darkness Character Generator

Character sheets for classic World of Darkness shapeshifters: Werewolf: the Apocalypse, Bastet, Mokolé, Ajaba, Ananasi, Corax, Gurahl, Kitsune, Nagah, Nuwisha, Ratkin and Rokea.

**Use it online:** https://kyphur.github.io/WoD_CharSheet/

**Use it offline:** download [WoD-Character-Generator.html](WoD-Character-Generator.html) (click it, then the download button) and double-click the file. Nothing to install.

## Making your character

1. At the top, set **Sheet** to **Player character (build it)**.
2. Pick your **Game**, then breed, auspice/aspect, tribe and gender (or leave any on Random).
3. Click **Start new character**. The **Character Creation Points** box counts your points as you click dots:
   - Attributes 7/5/3
   - Abilities 13/9/5 (no higher than 3 before freebies)
   - Backgrounds 5
   - starting Gifts
   - freebie points
4. Use the **+ Add** boxes for Backgrounds, Gifts, Merits and Flaws.
5. Click **Finish character** when everything is spent. Stuck? **Fill the rest randomly** fills in what's left.

## Keeping your character

- **Save character** downloads a small file. **Open character** loads it back next time.
- **Print / Save as PDF** prints a one-page sheet.

## Experience

After you finish, add experience at the bottom of the sheet ("Add experience"). Clicking a dot up spends XP at the book's costs, and lowering it gives the XP back.

Storytellers can switch **Sheet** to **NPC (random)** for instant random characters.

This is a fan-made tool. Game names, Gift names and trait values belong to their publishers. No rules text is included; you'll need the books to play.
